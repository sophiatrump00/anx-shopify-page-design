1,280Wh for the 12V loads that make an RV stop feel settled.

100Ah rated capacity, 1,280Wh rated energy and a 100A maximum continuous-discharge rating support a planned 12V auxiliary system, not engine starting. Use the runtime guide in the gallery as an estimate, then size the inverter, fusing and wiring around your actual loads.

Start with the tray, hold-down and cable path—not the label alone.

The 339 × 185 × 218mm case sets the starting point for an RV auxiliary system. Confirm tray size, hold-down clearance, terminal access and cable routing before ordering; the Group 31 name does not guarantee a universal drop-in fit.

Bluetooth status visibility from inside the RV.

Use compatible Bluetooth 5.0 monitoring to review state of charge, voltage, current and battery temperature before changing loads or charging sources. Follow the product manual and app instructions for the available functions.

21.16 lb makes installation—and the next service visit—easier.

At a listed 9.6kg / 21.16lb, the G31 is easier to move into service position than many comparably sized lead-acid batteries. Use the integrated carry strap for handling, then secure the case with a correctly sized tray and hold-down.

BMS and IP65 define the battery boundary; system design completes it.

The built-in BMS covers the listed current, voltage, temperature and short-circuit protections. IP65 means dust-tight and protected against water jets, not immersion; secure the battery upright and use correctly sized fuses, cables and terminal covers.

Low-temperature charge protection knows when to stop and resume.

Charging stops below 0°C battery temperature and resumes above 5°C. The listed operating ranges are 0–55°C for charging and -20–60°C for discharging; use the battery within the manual's environmental limits.

Four practical charging paths—when each uses the right equipment.

Plan LiFePO4-compatible CC/CV charge sources: an AC charger, solar with an MPPT controller, an alternator through a DC-to-DC charger, or a generator feeding a compatible charger. Do not connect sources directly to the battery terminals.

Expand from one G31 to a planned 4S / 4P system.

Use matched batteries of the same model, capacity and state of charge. The listed architecture supports up to 4 in series or 4 in parallel, and up to 16 matched batteries in a 4P4S bank; follow the manual for balancing, protection and cabling.

Frequently asked questions

What conditions apply to the cycle-life references?

The approved G31 reference tiers are 4,000+ cycles at 100% depth of discharge, 6,000 cycles at 80% DoD and 15,000 cycles at 60% DoD. Actual cycle life varies with charge and discharge rate, temperature, maintenance, depth of discharge and operating conditions.

Is Up to 10-Year Life the same as the 5-Year Limited Warranty?

No. Up to 10-Year Life is a product-life reference under suitable use. The 5-Year Limited Warranty covers eligible defects under the model-specific warranty terms; it does not guarantee ten years of product life.

What is included in 24/7 Support?

You can contact SuntNeew support for product setup, charging, Bluetooth, installation and after-sales questions. Provide the order information, model number and photos or readings relevant to the issue.

Can this battery be used to start an engine?

No. This is a deep-cycle auxiliary battery and is not intended to start a vehicle, boat engine or other device. Do not use it as a starting battery.

What should I confirm before installation?

Confirm the 339 × 185 × 218mm enclosure, terminal position, hold-down, upright mounting, cable route and service clearance. Size conductors, fuses, disconnects, inverter and charging sources for the complete DC system.

Are the appliance runtime figures guaranteed?

No. They are theoretical estimates based on 1,280Wh rated energy and the stated appliance loads. Actual runtime changes with duty cycle, inverter efficiency, battery condition, temperature and the real power draw of the connected equipment.

Can an IP65 battery be submerged?

No. IP65 means the enclosure is dust-tight and protected against water jets. It is not an immersion rating; keep the battery secured, upright and protected in accordance with the product manual.
